# Level #3 · IV — Treat your goals like a relationship

Goals for ai-company-os aren’t vibes. They’re commitments with a cost.

## Ask yourself

### How much do you love your goal?

Concrete goal examples:

- €1M with **1 avatar · 1 offer · 1 channel**  
- Become the default **AI Company OS** for European knowledge SMEs  
- Prove **perpetual local-model companies** beat BYO-agent control planes in your segment  

If you don’t love it enough to repeat the same boring install story for years, you’ll cheat with red-dress projects.

### What are you willing to give up to keep it?

| Give up | Why |
| ------- | --- |
| Building for ML teams / AI builders | Wrong avatar |
| Enterprise vanity logos early | Wrong urgency/sales cycle |
| Feature parity with Paperclip | Wrong battlefield |
| “Just one” custom platform deal | Breaks templates and air |
| Posting random AI news | Dilutes brand leverage |
| Switching primary channel every month | Kills compounding |

### Outlier outcomes need outlier sacrifice

Outlier for ai-company-os = category ownership in a niche others dismiss (“small models + file companies + boring SMEs”).

That likely costs:

- Saying no to exciting tech collaborations  
- Personal time on warm calls and installs before hiring  
- Restraint on product scope while competitors demo flashier agents  
- Geographic and industry discipline while money sits in Tier-2/3  

**Relationship test:** if a new idea doesn’t serve the locked avatar/offer/channel toward €1M, it’s an affair — not growth.
