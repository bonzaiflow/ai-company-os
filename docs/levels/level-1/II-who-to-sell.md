# Level #1 · II — Who to sell

Use the **3 P’s**, then qualify with the **Target Avatar** checklist. Detail lives in [`docs/ICPs.md`](../../ICPs.md).

## The 3 P’s (for ai-company-os)

### 1. Pain — someone with pain who is like you

Someone who already lives in LLM chat every day and feels the mess:

> “We use Claude every day. Everyone has their own prompts. Nobody knows which ones are good anymore.”

Same shape of pain you solve for yourself when building ai-company-os companies: scattered prompts → structured workforce with profiles, skills, tasks, audit.

### 2. Passion — something you are passionate about

Making **small models useful through structure** (narrow roles, short horizons, file state, owner control) — not chasing the biggest cloud model.

### 3. Profession — professional skill you have

Designing and running **agent companies**: planning org charts, skills, budgets, governance, delivery of reusable AI departments into knowledge businesses.

## Target Avatar checklist

Decision maker at a knowledge SME. Check all four before spending sales time.

- [ ] **They have the problem** — daily Claude/ChatGPT; prompts inconsistent; knowledge stuck in chats; juniors inconsistent; no AI governance
- [ ] **They have urgency** — growth or quality pressure: proposals, campaigns, candidates, reports can’t stay reinvented; AI chaos is costing margin *now*
- [ ] **They have the money** — ~€2M–15M revenue (stretch €1M–50M); already paying Claude Team / Max / Enterprise; can buy an install + retainer
- [ ] **They have authority to buy** — Founder, Managing Director, Partner, COO, Ops Director

### Ideal company (lock)

| Dimension | Target |
| --------- | ------ |
| Size | 10–50 employees |
| Revenue | €2M–15M |
| Type | Founder-led knowledge business |
| Geo | Europe — Tier 1: NL, DK, SE, FI, IE, LU |
| Eng | No dedicated software eng team (or &lt; ~20) |

### Tier-1 industries (sell first)

1. Management consulting  
2. Marketing agencies  
3. Recruitment agencies  

Then: accounting, law, architecture, engineering consultancies, executive coaches, fractional CFOs, real estate, HR consultancies.

### Disqualify fast

- Build AI products / have in-house ML  
- Large eng departments  
- Already on complex internal AI platforms  
- Manual/field work with little knowledge workflow  
- Solo freelancers without budget or authority  

### Buying signals

Claude Team/Max, ChatGPT Enterprise, AI on website/LinkedIn, hiring AI-related roles, founder posts about AI, heavy documentation business, &lt;100 employees, no eng team.
