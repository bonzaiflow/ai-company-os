# Level #1 · I — Knowledge, Skills, Environment

For ai-company-os, these three determine whether you can sell and deliver an AI Company OS.

## 1. Knowledge — what you know

| Domain | Why it matters for ai-company-os |
| ------ | ----------------------- |
| How knowledge firms actually work (proposals, delivery, reuse) | Buyers live in consulting, agencies, recruiting — not SaaS |
| Pain of LLM sprawl | Everyone has different prompts; knowledge dies in chats |
| Local / small-model economics | Perpetuity only works when inference is cheap or free |
| Difference vs Paperclip-class control planes | You sell *runtime + org*, not “bring your own agents” |
| ICP geography & buying signals | NL/DK/SE first; Claude Team, AI on site, founder AI posts |

**ai-company-os truth you must own:** companies that already use Claude daily, have little/no engineering, and have hit chaos instead of scale.

## 2. Skills — what you know how to do

| Skill | ai-company-os application |
| ----- | ---------------- |
| Diagnose chaotic AI usage in a 30-min call | Map chats → departments → employees → workflows |
| Design an agent company (roles, skills, budgets) | `ai-company-os plan` → org chart → launch |
| Install & onboard on their stack | Local Ollama and/or hosted; file-based company folder |
| Show proof fast | Demo company (e.g. ICP prospectors) producing real artifacts |
| Close without selling “AI agents” | Sell: *install an AI company* / *Claude as company OS* |

## 3. Environment — who and what you surround yourself with

| Surround yourself with | Avoid |
| ---------------------- | ----- |
| Founders of 10–50 person knowledge firms | Solopreneurs and freelancers (no budget / authority) |
| Operators drowning in docs, proposals, candidates, content | ML teams and AI-product builders (they build, don’t buy) |
| Peers who ship file-based / CLI-first tools | Hype circles that only talk agents, not delivery |
| Proof assets: live companies, audit logs, check-ins | Feature comparisons with Paperclip-style platforms |

**Rule:** if your calendar is full of people who *build* AI, you’re in the wrong room. Fill it with people who *suffer from* AI chaos.
