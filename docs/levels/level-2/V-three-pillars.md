# Level #2 · V — Build on the 3 pillars of business

Every ai-company-os company you sell has three pillars. Your *own* business does too — keep them balanced.

## 1. Acquisition — Marketing + Sales (“get them”)

| Piece | ai-company-os |
| ----- | ---- |
| Marketing | Free content + proof (demos, audits, before/after chaos) |
| Sales | Warm outreach primary; CLOSER; one offer one avatar |
| Lead getters | Customers → affiliates → employees → agencies (in that maturity order) |

Metric: qualified conversations with Tier-1 avatars who have problem + urgency + money + authority.

## 2. Delivery — Fulfillment / CS / Product (“Frontend”)

What the customer experiences:

| Piece | ai-company-os |
| ----- | ---- |
| Product | Runtime, UI, skills, planning, governance |
| Fulfillment | Plan → launch → schedule → first useful company output |
| Customer success | Check-ins answered, approvals timely, expansions suggested |
| Service | Install support, training managers who “manage” AI employees |

**Frontend promise:** they feel they *downloaded a department*, not bought a toolbox.

## 3. Operations — Finance, Legal, Payroll, HR, Recruiting (“Backend”)

| Piece | ai-company-os business |
| ----- | ------------- |
| Finance | Pricing, retainers, budgets (ironic: you sell budgets to customers — use them yourself) |
| Legal | Contracts, data handling for company folders, audit exports |
| Payroll / HR / Recruiting | Hiring closers and implementers with 3 D’s |

---

## Mirror insight

ai-company-os’s product *is* Acquisition + Delivery + Ops for an AI workforce (sales agents, fulfillment agents, etc.). Sell that mirror:

> We don’t give you better chat. We give your business the same three pillars — staffed by AI employees you control.

Your company must stay strong on all three. Acquisition without delivery creates churn; delivery without acquisition starves; ops without either creates busywork.
