# Level #2 · I — Get them to buy

Two jobs: **#1 you tell people about ai-company-os** · **#2 others tell people about ai-company-os**.

## Core Four (choose 1 primary until it works)

| Core Four | People who know you | People who don’t |
| --------- | ------------------- | ---------------- |
| **1:1 private** | Warm Outreach | Cold Outreach |
| **1:Many public** | Post free content | Run paid Ads |

### ai-company-os default stack (now)

| Priority | Channel | Why |
| -------- | ------- | --- |
| **#1 primary** | Warm outreach | Founder networks, past clients, LinkedIn connections in Tier-1 ICPs — highest trust for a novel OS install |
| **#2 support** | Free content | LinkedIn / short demos: chaotic Claude → AI company install; show live `companies/` + check-ins + audit |
| Later | Cold outreach | Only with a tight list (NL/DK consulting/agency/recruiting) + buying signals |
| Last | Paid ads | After creative + offer convert from warm/content; don’t buy traffic before message works |

**Rule:** pick **one** Core Four as primary. Content supports warm; don’t split energy across all four.

### What you tell them

Not “we built an agent orchestrator.”  
Tell: **your Claude usage is chaos → we install a company OS with departments, employees, budgets, and audit.**

Proof assets to attach:

- Live company demo (e.g. ICP prospectors)  
- Before/after: prompts in chats vs profiles + skills + tasks  
- One-liner from positioning vs Paperclip (perpetuity, audit, control, check-ins)

## Lead Getters (after you have customers)

| Lead Getters | People who know you | People who don’t |
| ------------ | ------------------- | ---------------- |
| **1:1 private** | Customers | Affiliates |
| **1:Many public** | Employees | Agencies |

### ai-company-os mapping

| Lead getter | How it shows up |
| ----------- | --------------- |
| **Customers** | Ask every happy install for intros to peer founders in the same industry |
| **Affiliates** | Fractional ops / AI-curious consultants who sell the install, you fulfill |
| **Employees** | (Later) SDRs / CS who run outbound and demos off the same 1 offer |
| **Agencies** | Boutique impl partners for accounting/law/etc. once Tier-1 is saturated |

Until you have paid customers, **you** are the only lead getter. Warm outreach + content first; then turn customers into the next lead source.
