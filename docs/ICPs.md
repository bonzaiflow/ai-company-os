# ICPs for AI Company OS

> **Target customer definition**
>
> Businesses that already rely on Claude (or similar LLMs) for daily operations, have little or no software engineering capability, and have reached the point where AI usage has become chaotic instead of scalable.
>
> AI Company OS turns scattered AI chats into a structured AI workforce.

---

# Tier 1 — Primary ICPs

These are the industries I would target first.

# Buying Signals

Companies likely ready for AI Company OS:

- Uses Claude Team or Claude Max
- Uses ChatGPT Enterprise
- AI mentioned on website
- AI mentioned in LinkedIn posts
- Hiring for AI-related roles
- Founder posts about AI
- Heavy documentation business
- Knowledge workers
- Less than 100 employees
- No dedicated software engineering team

---

# Disqualifiers

Avoid companies that:

- Build AI products themselves
- Have in-house ML teams
- Have software engineering departments larger than ~20 people
- Already use complex internal AI platforms
- Primarily perform manual or field work with minimal knowledge workflows

---

# Ideal Customer Persona

**Company**

- 10–50 employees
- €2M–15M revenue
- Founder-led
- European
- Knowledge business

**Decision Maker**

- Founder
- Managing Director
- Partner
- COO
- Operations Director

**Current Situation**

"We use Claude every day. Everyone has their own prompts. Nobody knows which ones are good anymore."

**Desired Future**

"Our company has standardized AI employees, documented workflows, reusable knowledge, and governance instead of hundreds of disconnected chats."
