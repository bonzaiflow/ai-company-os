# Companies hiring companies

The org chart stops at one company. Real leverage: companies delegating to other
companies — the same INBOX/task mechanics, one level up.

- **Contract tasks:** company A's chief can post a task to company B's queue
  (`createdBy: company-a`). B's result lands back in A's chief INBOX. Budget accounting
  charges A for B's spend (internal "invoice" in audit.jsonl).
- **Specialist companies as services:** a "WebResearch GmbH" with tuned skills and a
  remote 31b chief serves many small client companies that only orchestrate.
- **Templates / franchising:** `ai-company-os launch --template lead-gen` scaffolds a proven
  roster + skills; templates are just plan JSONs shipped like skills (zip upload works
  already).
- **Workspace org chart:** the home dashboard becomes a holding view — companies as
  nodes, contract flows as edges. The "senior-most agent" of the workspace is a natural
  next abstraction: one chat that can see all companies (the chief snapshot code
  already generalizes).
