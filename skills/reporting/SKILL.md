# Reporting

How to write a completion result your manager can act on:

- Lead with the outcome in one sentence ("Stored 14 verified leads in data/main.db table leads").
- List artifact locations: files under data/, database tables, notes in your workspace.
- Numbers over adjectives: counts, scores, URLs — not "many" or "great results".
- Name anything you could NOT do and why, in one line. Honesty beats polish.
- Keep the whole result under ~10 lines.
